# Round 0 report

Round 0 built the whole sample app: a local Leaflet map that shows license plate reader cameras from OpenStreetMap (Flock ones marked), lets you click a start and an end, and draws the fastest driving route next to one that avoids the cameras, with distance, time and cameras passed for each. All 12 tasks are done and merged, and all 591 tests pass with npm test. The visuals are still placeholders (plain colored circles and lines), offline mode uses made-up Atlanta cameras and a straight-line stub router, and the live Overpass and Valhalla paths have only been tested against faked servers, never the real ones. Nothing is blocked; 11 big guesses (app type, stack, data source, router, map tiles, name, and the 200 m Valhalla limit) and three new questions about detour length, trip privacy and the license wait for your answers. The next round would apply your answers, try the app end to end against the real servers, and then add whatever you pick next, such as address search or real map styling.
