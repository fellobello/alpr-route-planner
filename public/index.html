      About this map

      Coverage is incomplete. Camera locations are crowd-sourced and can be missing or out of date. A route that avoids every mapped camera can still pass unmapped ones.

      Cameras come from OpenStreetMap volunteers. If you know of a camera that is missing, you can add it to OpenStreetMap.
